Here is what to work on:

1. Idioms: tense, form and spelling (Parts 2 and 3).
- change hands → **changed** hands (the sentence uses “has recently…”)
- has a change → **had a change of heart**
- change out of all recognition → **has changed** out of all recognition
- change track → change **tack**
- has the blues → **have** the blues (the subject is “I”)
- blacked out → **black out** (it follows “appeared to”)

2. Verb patterns after reporting verbs (Parts 5 and 6).
- refused **to explain** (not “explaining”)
- denied **taking** (not “to take”)
- claimed **that the figures were accurate** (not “the figures being accurate”)
- promised **never to make** (not “promised not to make”, because you lose the meaning of “never”)

3. Giving advice tactfully (Part 7). 
- “If I were you, I would change your approach if I wanted better results” mixes I and you. Try “If I were you, **I’d try changing my approach** to get better results.”
- “…such decision” → “…such **a** decision”.

- Notes: this was your lowest score. A good note must keep the key information from the text, in as few words as possible. For example:

  - Set goals → work out the steps; one thing at a time = sense of achievement
  - Give yourself rewards → rewards for achievements increase motivation in future
  - Develop good habits → recognise good habits as they develop; work to maintain them
  - Have positive people around you → positive people support you and motivate you
