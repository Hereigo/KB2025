#### Wishes about the Present.

- I **wish** I **lived** by the sea. (but I don't.)
- I **wish** you **were** here. (but you aren't.)
- She **wishes** she **could** drive. (but she can't.)

#### Wishes about annoying things

- I **wish** my neighbours **would** be quite at night. (they don't and it annoys me.)
- I **wish** you **wouldn't** call me at weekend. (you often call me and I don't like.)
- I **wish** it **would** stop raining. (it's raining and I'm fed up.)

#### Wishes about the Past

- I **wish** **I'd gone** to university when I was younger. (but I didn't go.)
- I **wish** they **hadn't moved** away. (but they moved away.)
- I **wish** I **hadn't eaten** so much. (but I ate too much.)
- He **wishes** he **had studied** harder for the exam. (but he didn't study hard.)

#### If only ...

- **If only** I **lived** by the sea **!** ("I wish I lived ...")
- **If only** he **would be** quite **!** ("I wish he would be ...")
- **If only** I **had listened** to my mother **!** ("I wish I'd listened to my ...")
- **If only** the car **would** start **!** ("I wish the car would start.")

#### Should(n't) have (regret about smth.)

- We **should have** arrived earlier. Now there aren't seats. (now we regret it.)
- I **shouldn't have** spoken to you like that. Sorry. (now I regret it.)
- You **should have** told me about the problem earlier! (now I regret you didn't.)
