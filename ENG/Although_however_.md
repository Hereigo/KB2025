### Position and punctuation of however and though

| Position  | Example                                 |
| --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Beginning | **However ,** I'm not very good at it.  |
| Middle    | I'm **, however ,** not very good at it.|
| End       | I'm not very good at it **, however**.  |
| Though at the beginning | **Though** he is talented, he needs practice. |
| Though at the end | He needs practice **, though**. |
