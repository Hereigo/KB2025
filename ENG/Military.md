## Military service

#### - Have a military deferment (postponement)

#### - Be deferred from mobilisation (delayed until)

#### - Be extempt from mobilisation (be free from)

#### - Be reserved for essential work

---

|              | Deferred | Postponed |
| ------------ | -------- | --------- |
| *Definition* | Something that is delayed or put off until a later time | Something that is delayed or rescheduled to a later time |
| *Timing* | Usually refers to a specific action or decision that is delayed | Can refer to a variety of things being delayed, such as events or tasks |
| *Implications* | May imply a deliberate choice to delay something | May imply a temporary delay due to external factors |

---

( **Implications** - Consequences, hidden meaning or involvement )
